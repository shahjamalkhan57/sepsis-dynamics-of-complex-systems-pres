Understanding sepsis

Sepsis is a life-threatening condition in which the body’s response to an infection causes organ dysfunction. This simulation shows one possible sequence of changes inside a blood vessel over an illustrative 48-hour period.

Watch how immune cells, the vessel lining and blood flow change. Pause or hover to explore. Select Start treatment to compare earlier and later intervention. The timing and outcomes are examples, not medical predictions.
